\begin{itemize}
  \item Four frameworks compose; we do not pick one
    (leak class $\times$ observer ring; evidence layers A--D; evidence levels
    L0--L4; the differential $2{\times}2$ attribution).
  \item The observer-ring vocabulary (canonical grid:
    \texttt{prototypes/fcvd\_ct/artifact/attacker\_profile.py}):
    \begin{itemize}
      \item O0 public transcript $\rightarrow$ O7 invasive observer, ordered by
        directness of reach; ``proved at O2, carried outward to O1'' is
        meaningful because of the ordering; O6-and-inward is moot rather than
        uncovered.
    \end{itemize}
  \item The asset axis the rings do not carry: weights (static --- repetition
    wins) vs activations/prompts (fresh per query --- one-shot channels only)
    vs structure (sparsity, shapes, architecture --- leaked by control
    structure, not values).
  \item What ``secure'' means in this paper: a claim is fully specified only
    with all four coordinates (observer, evidence kind, evidence level,
    attribution).
  \item Scope / non-goals: integrity of weights, TEE partitioning, and running
    the compiled model are out (issues \#29, \#34, \#36).
\end{itemize}

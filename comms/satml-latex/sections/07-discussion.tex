\section{Discussion and Limitations}

% OUTLINE ONLY.

\begin{itemize}
  \item What the evidence does not reach: measured results are machine- and
    version-specific (Zen5 confound as the worked example); proofs are in a
    model, not on silicon (layers A--D vocabulary keeps these apart).
  \item Model-blocked steps: the two parallel Polygeist passes; what
    unblocking would take.
  \item The compiler-removed surprise: compilation is not monotonically bad
    for confidentiality --- implications for ``compile with hardening''
    guidance.
  \item Generalizing past Polygeist/Inductor: what transfers via the shared
    dialects, what is compiler-specific.
  \item Ethics: no deployed-system exploitation; all measurement on our own
    hardware and models.
\end{itemize}

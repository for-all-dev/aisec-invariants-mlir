Leak class against observer layer

Two axes. What leaks — the kind of secret-dependent thing the program did, and that list closes at four. Who is watching — how directly the observer reaches secret state. Every intersection carries a verdict, because a blank cell is an opinion nobody stated.

The blue block is what the MLIR pipeline closes: the functional and timing classes, out to O2. Timing is proved at O2 and carried outward — O2 sees strictly more than O1, so runs that are indistinguishable in the constant-time trace are indistinguishable to any weaker observer. Further in, the classes stop being the binding constraint: an observer holding process memory does not need to infer anything.

Method

A semi-automatic compiler verifier

It proves that the MLIR lowering steps from source code down to LLVM do not turn safe code into unsafe code. "Compiler" here means any MLIR compiler.

"Safe" means functional equivalence together with resistance to timing attacks. Other kinds of vulnerability — cache, power draw — are out of scope.

The method of choice is FCVD: it translates dialects into SMT and, once every translation is present, gives a safety guarantee through self-composition of the SMT. The catch is the "once every translation is present" part: each MLIR operation on the path needs a mapping into SMT.

What makes it semi-automatic is two things:

  1. a translation between two levels is written once — over time every common transformation is one that has already been paid for;
  2. where no SMT mapping exists, a macro-template takes its place — a declarative rule of the form "a loop with bounds [S, E, St] and body F(x) is equivalent to a four-block cf.br skeleton", proved once and applied thereafter without re-proof.

Every proof is therefore produced by the same algorithm, and bringing a new compiler into the fold is a matter of following it.

Attacker profile

What the proofs are against

Before any of the compilers below, the question of who is being kept out. Two axes: what leaks — the kind of secret-dependent thing the program did, and that list closes at four — and who is watching, how directly the observer reaches secret state. Every intersection carries a verdict, because a blank cell is an opinion nobody stated.

The rings are ordered strictly by directness of access, which puts every observer who infers outside every observer who reads: a probe on the power line sits closer to the switching activity than a cache does, but further from the secret than a debugger holding the memory. Sketches of this onion usually draw the physical observer innermost, and then the order stops matching what the picture says it shows.

The blue block is what the MLIR pipeline closes. Timing is proved at O2 and carried outward, but only as far as the carry is sound: a weaker observer inherits the result when its observation is a function of the O2 trace, which holds for end-to-end latency and stops at the first observer whose clock resolves what the model leaves out. At O6 and inward the classes stop being the binding constraint altogether — an observer holding process memory has nothing left to infer. One cell breaks the pattern, and it is worth the click.

    Six compilers

    How much of each can be checked today

    The first three were picked because they carry few "large" types and lowerings; the work started there, and they are the only ones with proved steps. The other three are measurements rather than verifications: a GPU compiler, a neural-network front end and a C front end. The count comes from each compiler's own test corpus — how many distinct operations occur, and what share of the mentions falls on the ones we can translate.

    What is actually measured

    Measurements, not estimates

    Every template was run once in this same process; the time covers building both SMT queries and z3's answers. A step is verified only if it preserves constant-time and still computes the same thing — the two are independent, and the second column is the half a leakage property cannot see.
